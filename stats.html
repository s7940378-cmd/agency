How to play Operation: Agency

Drop in. Ride the bus over the island and jump out wherever you like. Skydive, then your glider opens near the ground.
Loot up. Open chests, grab guns, shields and heals. Rarer weapons glow brighter colours.
Build. Harvest wood, brick and metal with your pickaxe, then build walls, ramps, floors and cones to win fights.
Ride. Helicopters seat four - passengers can shoot out of them. Cars seat two. Ram an enemy and they go flying.
Beat the storm. The storm eye shrinks through the match. Stay inside it and be the last agent (or team) standing.
Play with friends. Make a party and play duos, squads, 25 v 25 - or face your friends in 1 v 1 and 2 v 2.

FAQ

Is it free? Yes. Make a free account to play as many matches as you like and keep your level, locker and stats.

Does it work on a Chromebook? Yes - it was built to run smoothly on Chromebooks. Lower the graphics setting if it feels slow.

What's in the Item Shop? New outfits, pickaxes, emotes, gliders and contrails every day. Most cost coins you earn by playing.

Where are my stats saved? In your account in this browser. Stats from another computer show up after you move your account there with a transfer code.
